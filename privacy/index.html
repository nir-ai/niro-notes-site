Niro Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

In short: Niro Notes does not collect any data. Your notes stay on your iPad, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to share it.

Who we are

This policy covers the Niro Notes app for iPad, published by the developer of Niro Notes (“we”).

What the app collects

Nothing. Niro Notes has no accounts or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. The app does not connect to the internet and does not send your notes or any other information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPad

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your iPad, inside the app’s own storage:

  • your folders, notes and handwriting;
  • the copies of PDFs you import (the app keeps its own copy so the note still works if the original is moved);
  • your settings, such as pen colors, thickness and sort order.

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, photos, contacts, location or microphone.

What you choose to share

Files move in or out of the app only when you do it yourself, for example:

  • exporting a note as a PDF and sending it through the share sheet (AirDrop, Mail, Files and so on);
  • opening or dragging a PDF into Niro Notes from another app.

Where a shared file goes, and how it is handled there, is up to you and to the app or service you pick.

Apple services

We use no third-party services. Some features of your iPad are provided by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy:

  • Device backups. Like other app data, your notes can be included in your iCloud or computer backups, if you have turned them on.
  • The share sheet and the apps you share to.
  • Crash reports and feedback. If you test the app through TestFlight and send feedback, or allow your iPad to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass crash reports and that feedback on to us. These do not include your notes.

Children

Niro Notes does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a note or folder in the app to remove it, and its files, from your iPad. Deleting the app removes all of its data. Copies in your own device backups stay there until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
